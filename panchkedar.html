Temples Covered

Kedarnath

The holiest among Panch Kedar, Kedarnath temple is set against the stunning backdrop of the mighty Kedarnath range. Lord Shiva is worshiped here as the 'Lord of the Field'.

Tungnath

The highest Shiva temple in the world, believed to be built by the Pandavas to seek forgiveness from Lord Shiva.

Rudranath

Set amidst a dense forest, Rudranath is where Shiva's face is worshiped. The trek to Rudranath offers breathtaking beauty.

Madhyamaheshwar

Here the navel (nabhi) of Lord Shiva is worshiped. Known for its peaceful location surrounded by snow-capped peaks.

Kalpeshwar

The only Panch Kedar temple accessible year-round, where Lord Shiva is worshiped in his hair form (jata).
