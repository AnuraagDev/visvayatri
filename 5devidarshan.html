Temples Covered

Chintpurni Devi

Relieve your worries at this sacred Shakti Peetha where Goddess Sati's feet fell.

Jwala Ji

Home to the eternal flame that has burned without fuel for centuries — a true wonder!

Kangra Devi

Seek blessings at Brajeshwari Temple, a Shakti Peetha with a rich spiritual heritage.

Chamunda Devi

Visit the fierce form of Goddess Durga in this serene riverside temple.

Baglamukhi Devi

Goddess of victory, Baglamukhi Devi's temple is powerful for devotees seeking success.

Itinerary Overview

  • Day 1: Pathankot → Chintpurni Devi → Jwala Ji
  • Day 2: Kangra Devi → Chamunda Devi → Baglamukhi Devi
  • Day 3: Return to Pathankot
