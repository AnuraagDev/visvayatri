Vaishno Devi Yatra

Visit the sacred shrine of Maa Vaishno Devi nestled in the Trikuta Mountains. This spiritual pilgrimage attracts millions of devotees annually seeking blessings and peace.

Highlights:

  • Darshan of the holy cave and naturally formed pindis
  • Trekking experience with scenic views
  • Helicopter / pony / palki facilities available

Itinerary Overview:

  • Day 1: Arrival at Katra, hotel check-in
  • Day 2: Early morning yatra to Bhawan and darshan
  • Day 3: Return from Bhawan → Katra → Departure
